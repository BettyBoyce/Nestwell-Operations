# Nestwell media library

Photos and videos of Nestwell properties, the garden, the team and the family behind the business. Use them for listings, the website, social posts and turnover reference.

**How files are stored:** photos are resized to 2000px on the long side. Videos are compressed to 720p MP4 so the repo stays small. The full-quality originals stay on Betty's phone and in her cloud storage. Ask Betty if you need an original.

**Privacy rule:** this repo is public. Never add photos or videos of children who aren't fully dressed (diapers, swimsuits, bath time). Use only photos and graphics we took ourselves, not ones reposted from other accounts.
