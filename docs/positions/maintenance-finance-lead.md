# Nestwell Stays LLC — Official Position Description

| | |
|---|---|
| **Position Title** | Maintenance & Finance Lead |
| **Department** | Property Operations & Finance |
| **Business Type** | Mid-Term Rental Property Management |
| **Position Holder** | Brandon Boyce |

## Position Summary
The Maintenance & Finance Lead is responsible for overseeing the physical maintenance, repair coordination, and financial administration of Nestwell Stays' mid-term rental portfolio. This position ensures that properties remain safe, functional, well-maintained, and ready for occupancy while maintaining timely financial obligations and accurate reporting to the company's bookkeeper.

The role combines hands-on property maintenance with contractor oversight and financial management to protect company assets, control operating expenses, and support a positive resident experience.

## Primary Duties & Responsibilities

### 1. Property Maintenance & Repairs
- Perform routine maintenance, minor repairs, and general property upkeep across the rental portfolio.
- Diagnose maintenance issues and determine whether repairs can be completed personally or require a qualified contractor.
- Address maintenance requests in a timely manner, prioritizing safety, property protection, and resident comfort.
- Conduct periodic property inspections to identify maintenance needs, potential hazards, and preventative repair opportunities.
- Maintain and organize tools, equipment, and maintenance supplies.
- Support property readiness between resident stays by addressing repair needs and ensuring all major systems and amenities are functional.

### 2. Contractor Coordination & Oversight
- Identify, hire, and coordinate qualified contractors or service providers when repairs exceed personal capabilities, require specialized licensing, or cannot be completed within a reasonable timeframe.
- Obtain estimates and evaluate contractor pricing, availability, and scope of work.
- Schedule contractor visits and coordinate access to occupied or vacant properties.
- Oversee completion of contracted work and verify satisfactory results.
- Maintain relationships with reliable service providers, including plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, landscapers, and other trades.
- Communicate significant repairs, anticipated costs, and maintenance concerns to ownership.

### 3. Financial Administration & Bill Payment
- Oversee the timely payment of all business-related financial obligations, including mortgages, utilities, insurance, property taxes, vendor invoices, and other operating expenses.
- Monitor recurring expenses and payment schedules to prevent late fees, service interruptions, or missed financial obligations.
- Review invoices and statements for accuracy before authorizing payment.
- Maintain organized records of business expenditures, receipts, invoices, and payment confirmations.
- Monitor operating expenses and identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary costs without compromising property quality.
- Communicate upcoming significant expenses and financial concerns to ownership.

### 4. Bookkeeping Coordination & Financial Reporting
- Serve as the primary point of contact for the company's bookkeeper regarding expenses, payments, and financial documentation.
- Provide accurate and timely financial records, receipts, invoices, and transaction details for bookkeeping and reconciliation.
- Respond to bookkeeping questions and assist in resolving discrepancies.
- Ensure property-related expenditures are properly documented and attributable to the appropriate rental property.
- Support the preparation of accurate financial reports by providing requested information and clarification.
- Coordinate with ownership and the bookkeeper to maintain clear financial visibility across the rental portfolio.

### 5. Asset Protection & Preventative Maintenance
- Identify and recommend preventative maintenance measures to extend the useful life of property systems, appliances, fixtures, and equipment.
- Monitor the condition of major property components, including roofing, plumbing, electrical systems, HVAC, and exterior structures.
- Assist with planning and coordinating larger repairs, replacements, and capital improvements.
- Respond appropriately to urgent property maintenance situations to minimize damage and disruption.
- Support long-term property value through consistent upkeep and responsible expense management.

## Authority & Accountability
The Maintenance & Finance Lead is authorized to perform routine maintenance, coordinate necessary repairs, engage appropriate contractors within ownership-approved spending parameters, and administer recurring business payments.

Major capital expenditures, significant property improvements, and nonroutine financial commitments require ownership approval.

The position is accountable for ensuring maintenance needs are addressed, contractors are appropriately managed, financial obligations are paid on time, and accurate supporting documentation is provided to the bookkeeper.

## Performance Expectations
Successful performance in this position is demonstrated through:

- **Property Condition:** Rental properties remain safe, operational, and well-maintained.
- **Maintenance Responsiveness:** Repair requests are addressed promptly and appropriately.
- **Contractor Management:** Outsourced work is completed to acceptable standards and at reasonable costs.
- **Financial Reliability:** Business bills and recurring financial obligations are paid accurately and on time.
- **Recordkeeping:** Financial documents and expense records are organized and provided to the bookkeeper consistently.
- **Cost Management:** Maintenance and operating expenses are managed responsibly to protect profitability.
- **Communication:** Ownership remains informed of significant maintenance issues, financial concerns, and anticipated expenditures.

## Overall Position Objective
To protect the physical and financial health of Nestwell Stays' rental portfolio through dependable maintenance, effective contractor management, timely bill payment, and organized financial administration—supporting profitable operations and a consistent, high-quality resident experience.
