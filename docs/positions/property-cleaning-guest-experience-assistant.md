# Nestwell Stays LLC — Official Position Description

| | |
|---|---|
| **Position Title** | Property Cleaning & Guest Experience Assistant |
| **Department** | Property Operations & Guest Services |
| **Business Type** | Mid-Term Rental Property Management |
| **Position Holder** | Maxine Boyce |

## Position Summary
The Property Cleaning & Guest Experience Assistant is responsible for supporting the cleanliness, organization, presentation, and overall resident experience of Nestwell Stays' mid-term rental portfolio.

The position primarily focuses on routine property cleaning, restocking household and guest supplies, and maintaining the company's standards for clean, comfortable, and well-equipped rental accommodations.

Additional responsibilities may include assisting with unit turnovers, conducting property inspections, organizing and tracking supply inventory, uploading financial documentation, and providing feedback and recommendations to improve the guest experience.

This role directly contributes to property readiness, operational efficiency, resident satisfaction, and the consistent delivery of the Nestwell Stays hospitality standard.

## Primary Duties & Responsibilities

### 1. Property Cleaning & Housekeeping
- Perform routine cleaning of assigned rental units, common areas, and designated property spaces.
- Clean and sanitize kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, bedrooms, and other designated surfaces.
- Dust furniture, clean mirrors and fixtures, vacuum carpets, and sweep or mop floors as needed.
- Assist with laundering, folding, and organizing linens, towels, and other household textiles.
- Maintain cleanliness and organization of storage closets, cleaning supply areas, and housekeeping equipment.
- Identify and report damaged furnishings, missing items, excessive wear, or maintenance concerns.
- Follow established cleaning procedures to ensure consistency across rental properties.
- Properly use, store, and maintain cleaning products and equipment in accordance with safety guidelines.

### 2. Guest Supply Stocking & Replenishment
- Monitor and replenish designated household and guest supplies at rental properties.
- Restock essential items such as toiletries, paper products, cleaning supplies, laundry products, and other provided amenities.
- Ensure supplies are neatly organized, appropriately presented, and accessible to residents.
- Identify low-stock items and communicate replenishment needs to management.
- Assist with preparing welcome supplies and other guest amenities as directed.
- Maintain consistency in supply presentation across Nestwell Stays properties.
- Help minimize unnecessary waste and overstocking through responsible supply management.

## Secondary & As-Assigned Responsibilities

### 3. Unit Turnover & Property Readiness
- Assist with preparing rental units between resident departures and new arrivals.
- Complete assigned turnover cleaning and restocking tasks according to company checklists.
- Inspect furniture, linens, appliances, and amenities for cleanliness and presentation.
- Assist with staging and arranging furnishings, decor, and household essentials.
- Verify that designated guest supplies and amenities are available before occupancy.
- Report outstanding cleaning, maintenance, or readiness concerns to management.
- Support timely completion of turnovers to minimize vacancy periods and maintain resident satisfaction.

### 4. Property Inspections & Quality Assurance
- Conduct assigned visual inspections using established property inspection checklists.
- Evaluate cleanliness, organization, supply levels, and general property presentation.
- Identify visible damage, missing items, maintenance concerns, or conditions requiring management attention.
- Document inspection findings with notes and photographs when requested.
- Communicate inspection results to ownership or designated management.
- Assist with verifying that corrective actions have been completed.
- Support consistent property quality standards throughout the rental portfolio.

### 5. Supply Inventory & Organization
- Review existing inventories of cleaning products, household supplies, linens, and guest amenities.
- Organize supply storage areas to maintain cleanliness, accessibility, and efficient use of space.
- Assist with counting and documenting available inventory.
- Identify damaged, expired, missing, or low-stock items.
- Recommend improvements to supply organization and replenishment procedures.
- Assist with preparing supply lists and communicating purchasing needs to management.
- Help reduce unnecessary purchases by monitoring existing inventory and proper product usage.

### 6. Administrative & Bookkeeping Support
- Upload receipts, invoices, and other business-related documents into the company's designated bookkeeping or document management system.
- Ensure documents are legible, complete, and appropriately categorized or associated with the correct rental property.
- Assist with organizing electronic records and maintaining accurate documentation.
- Report missing information, duplicate records, or documentation concerns to management.
- Maintain confidentiality when handling company financial or operational information.

### 7. Guest Experience Consulting & Hospitality Improvement
- Evaluate rental properties from the perspective of an incoming or existing resident.
- Provide feedback regarding property comfort, cleanliness, functionality, decor, organization, and overall presentation.
- Identify opportunities to improve the resident experience through thoughtful amenities, convenience, and attention to detail.
- Assist with evaluating welcome supplies, household essentials, and other hospitality offerings.
- Recommend improvements to furniture arrangements, storage solutions, and the usability of rental spaces.
- Participate in discussions regarding guest feedback and potential property enhancements.
- Support the development and refinement of Nestwell Stays' hospitality and property presentation standards.
- Communicate observations and recommendations to management for consideration and approval.

## Authority & Accountability
The Property Cleaning & Guest Experience Assistant is authorized to independently complete assigned routine cleaning, restocking, and supply organization duties in accordance with established company procedures.

Additional responsibilities, including unit turnovers, property inspections, bookkeeping documentation, and guest experience consulting, are performed as assigned by ownership or designated management.

The position may provide recommendations regarding supplies, amenities, furnishings, and property improvements but does not independently authorize significant purchases, property modifications, or financial commitments.

All work must be performed using appropriate safety procedures, approved equipment, and applicable supervision requirements.

## Performance Expectations
Successful performance in this position is demonstrated through:

- **Cleaning Quality:** Assigned rental units and property areas consistently meet company cleanliness standards.
- **Supply Readiness:** Essential household and guest supplies remain appropriately stocked, organized, and available.
- **Turnover Efficiency:** Assigned turnover responsibilities are completed accurately and within established timeframes.
- **Property Presentation:** Rental accommodations remain welcoming, orderly, and consistent with company hospitality standards.
- **Inventory Management:** Supplies are organized, monitored, and used responsibly to minimize waste and unnecessary expenses.
- **Inspection Accuracy:** Property concerns and deficiencies are identified, documented, and communicated promptly.
- **Administrative Accuracy:** Assigned receipts and business documents are uploaded accurately and in a timely manner.
- **Guest Experience Contribution:** Provides thoughtful, practical feedback that supports improvements in resident comfort and satisfaction.
- **Communication & Accountability:** Completes assigned responsibilities reliably and communicates concerns, progress, and recommendations to management.

## Overall Position Objective
To support the operational success of Nestwell Stays LLC by maintaining clean, organized, well-stocked, and welcoming rental properties while contributing to efficient property turnovers, inventory management, quality assurance, and continuous improvements to the resident experience.

Through dependable execution of assigned responsibilities and thoughtful attention to hospitality standards, this position helps protect property value, strengthen resident satisfaction, and support the company's commitment to providing comfortable, high-quality mid-term rental accommodations.
