# Nest Well Stays LLC — Official Position Description

| | |
|---|---|
| **Position Title** | Guest Experience Artist & Hospitality Apprentice |
| **Department** | Guest Services & Property Operations |
| **Business Type** | Mid-Term Rental Property Management |
| **Position Holder** | Renlee Boyce |

## Position Summary
The Guest Experience Artist & Hospitality Apprentice supports Nest Well Stays LLC by contributing original artwork, personalized guest welcome materials, and creative ideas that enhance the comfort and hospitality experience across the company's mid-term rental portfolio.

The position primarily focuses on creating handmade welcome cards for incoming residents, producing original artwork for display within rental properties, and helping select thoughtful snacks and welcome amenities.

As an employee in training, the position also introduces foundational hospitality and property management skills through supervised participation in unit turnovers, property presentation, and guest experience improvement activities.

This role encourages creativity, responsibility, attention to detail, and an understanding of how personalized hospitality contributes to a welcoming rental experience.

## Primary Duties & Responsibilities

### 1. Guest Welcome Cards & Personalized Artwork
- Design, draw, paint, or decorate handmade welcome cards for incoming rental guests.
- Create original artwork that reflects the welcoming and thoughtful hospitality standards of Nest Well Stays.
- Prepare welcome cards in advance of scheduled guest arrivals.
- Maintain a consistent standard of care, creativity, and presentation in completed cards.
- Assist with placing completed welcome cards in designated locations within rental units.
- Organize and properly care for company-provided art materials and supplies.
- Explore new creative designs and seasonal themes appropriate for guest welcome materials.

### 2. Original Artwork & Property Decor
- Create original drawings, paintings, and other age-appropriate artwork for display within Nest Well Stays rental properties.
- Contribute creative ideas for artwork that complements the style, atmosphere, and personality of individual rental units.
- Assist with selecting appropriate artwork for designated property spaces.
- Help prepare completed artwork for framing or display under management supervision.
- Participate in periodic updates to property artwork and decorative elements.
- Support the company's goal of creating distinctive, welcoming, and thoughtfully designed rental accommodations.

### 3. Guest Welcome Snacks & Hospitality Amenities
- Assist with selecting snacks and treats for guest welcome baskets or designated hospitality areas.
- Provide recommendations for snack varieties that may appeal to different guest preferences.
- Help assemble and organize approved welcome snacks and amenities.
- Assist with checking snack packaging, presentation, and expiration dates.
- Participate in discussions about improving welcome gifts and other thoughtful guest touches.
- Help ensure guest welcome offerings are attractive, organized, and ready for arrival.

## Employee Training & Development Responsibilities

### 4. Unit Turnover Training & Assistance
- Participate in supervised, age-appropriate property turnover activities.
- Learn the importance of cleanliness, organization, and property readiness between guest stays.
- Assist with simple turnover tasks such as arranging pillows, organizing approved amenities, placing welcome cards, and preparing guest supplies.
- Observe and learn company cleaning and turnover procedures.
- Practice completing assigned tasks using simplified property readiness checklists.
- Develop an understanding of how attention to detail contributes to a positive guest experience.
- Follow all safety instructions and perform training activities under appropriate adult supervision.

### 5. Guest Experience & Comfort Improvement
- Evaluate rental spaces from a guest's perspective and share ideas for making properties more comfortable and welcoming.
- Provide creative suggestions for decor, amenities, welcome gifts, and property presentation.
- Participate in age-appropriate discussions about hospitality and guest satisfaction.
- Assist with testing the comfort and usability of approved household items and guest amenities.
- Help identify small details that could make a guest's stay more enjoyable.
- Learn how thoughtful hospitality and personalized touches contribute to the Nest Well Stays brand.

### 6. Hospitality & Business Education
- Learn foundational concepts of operating a mid-term rental business.
- Develop an understanding of the importance of customer service, cleanliness, reliability, and teamwork.
- Practice following instructions, completing assigned projects, and meeting reasonable deadlines.
- Learn basic concepts related to supplies, inventory, quality standards, and guest satisfaction.
- Develop creativity, organization, communication, and problem-solving skills through practical business activities.
- Participate in supervised learning opportunities designed to introduce future property management responsibilities.

## Authority & Accountability
The Guest Experience Artist & Hospitality Apprentice is authorized to complete assigned creative projects, including handmade welcome cards, original artwork, and approved guest welcome preparations.

All property-related training, turnover assistance, and guest experience activities are performed under appropriate adult supervision.

Artwork placement, guest amenity purchases, and changes to property presentation require management approval.

Assigned duties must remain age-appropriate, with an emphasis on safety, creative development, practical learning, and meaningful contributions to the business.

## Performance Expectations
Successful performance in this position is demonstrated through:

- **Welcome Card Completion:** Handmade guest welcome cards are thoughtfully prepared for assigned guest arrivals.
- **Creative Contribution:** Original artwork is completed and contributes to the welcoming atmosphere of rental properties.
- **Guest Hospitality:** Participates in selecting and preparing thoughtful guest welcome amenities.
- **Attention to Detail:** Demonstrates care in the presentation of artwork, welcome materials, and assigned hospitality tasks.
- **Training Participation:** Shows continued progress in understanding property turnovers and basic rental operations.
- **Creativity & Initiative:** Contributes original ideas for improving guest comfort and property presentation.
- **Responsibility:** Completes assigned projects within reasonable, age-appropriate timeframes.
- **Safety & Teamwork:** Follows instructions, works cooperatively, and participates safely in supervised activities.

## Overall Position Objective
To enhance the Nest Well Stays guest experience through original artwork, personalized welcome cards, thoughtful hospitality touches, and creative contributions to rental property presentation.

As a Hospitality Apprentice, this position also provides an opportunity to develop foundational business skills, responsibility, and an understanding of rental property operations through supervised, age-appropriate learning and participation.

The role supports Nest Well Stays LLC's commitment to creating comfortable, memorable, and welcoming accommodations that feel like a home rather than simply a place to stay.
