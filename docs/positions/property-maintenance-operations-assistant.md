## Position Summary
The Property Maintenance & Operations Assistant is responsible for supporting the routine upkeep, cleanliness, and operational readiness of Nestwell Stays' mid-term rental portfolio.

The position primarily focuses on waste management, recycling services, lawn mowing, and landscape trimming to ensure rental properties maintain a clean, attractive, and well-maintained appearance.

Additional responsibilities may be assigned as business needs arise, including property cleaning, supply transportation, administrative support, bookkeeping documentation, rental management education, and assistance with tenant application reviews.

## Primary Duties & Responsibilities

### 1. Waste Management & Recycling
- Collect and properly dispose of trash and recyclable materials from designated rental property areas.
- Ensure waste and recycling materials are placed in the appropriate containers according to local disposal guidelines.
- Move garbage and recycling containers to designated curbside collection locations on scheduled pickup days.
- Return containers to their appropriate storage locations promptly following collection.
- Monitor containers for overflow, improper disposal, or maintenance concerns.
- Maintain cleanliness and organization of waste storage areas.
- Report unusual waste accumulation, damaged containers, or other concerns to management.

### 2. Lawn Care & Landscape Maintenance
- Perform routine lawn mowing at assigned rental properties.
- Trim grass and vegetation around walkways, fences, landscaping features, and other designated areas.
- Maintain clean and presentable lawn edges and outdoor spaces.
- Remove or properly dispose of grass clippings and minor landscaping debris.
- Monitor lawn and landscaping conditions and report irrigation concerns, overgrowth, or potential maintenance needs.
- Safely operate, clean, and properly store lawn maintenance tools and equipment.
- Complete lawn maintenance on an appropriate seasonal schedule to maintain property appearance and curb appeal.

## Secondary & As-Assigned Responsibilities

### 3. Property Cleaning & Turnover Assistance
- Assist with general property cleaning and preparation between resident stays when requested.
- Support cleaning of interior or exterior common areas, storage spaces, and other designated locations.
- Assist with removing trash, organizing supplies, and preparing properties for incoming residents.
- Report damaged, missing, or malfunctioning items identified during cleaning or property visits.

### 4. Supply & Material Transportation
- Transport cleaning supplies, maintenance materials, household items, and other business-related materials between rental properties or designated locations.
- Assist with loading, unloading, and organizing supplies and equipment.
- Support inventory organization and restocking activities as assigned.
- Handle company property and materials responsibly to prevent loss or damage.

### 5. Administrative & Bookkeeping Support
- Upload receipts, invoices, and other expense documentation into the company's designated bookkeeping or recordkeeping system.
- Ensure uploaded documentation is legible, complete, and accurately associated with the appropriate transaction or property.
- Assist with organizing administrative records and operational documentation.
- Notify management of missing receipts, incomplete information, or documentation concerns.

### 6. Rental Management Education & Professional Development
- Participate in assigned educational activities related to rental property management and business operations.
- Develop familiarity with mid-term rental operations, resident services, property maintenance, and financial fundamentals.
- Learn company procedures, operational systems, and property management best practices.
- Apply newly acquired knowledge to assigned responsibilities under appropriate supervision.
- Demonstrate continued growth in understanding the responsibilities associated with operating a rental property business.

### 7. Tenant Application Review Support
- Assist management with the preliminary review of prospective tenant applications when assigned.
- Review application completeness and identify missing or inconsistent information.
- Become familiar with company rental qualification standards and the importance of consistent, fair application procedures.
- Maintain confidentiality of prospective tenant information and supporting documentation.
- Refer application findings to management for evaluation and final decisions.
- Follow applicable fair housing requirements and company screening procedures. Final applicant approval or denial remains the responsibility of authorized management.

## Authority & Accountability
The Property Maintenance & Operations Assistant is authorized to independently complete assigned routine waste management and lawn maintenance duties within established company procedures.

Additional responsibilities, including cleaning, transportation, financial documentation, and tenant application review, are performed as directed by ownership or designated management.

The position does not independently authorize business expenditures, engage contractors, or make final tenant screening or leasing decisions.

All work must be completed using appropriate safety practices, company equipment procedures, and applicable supervision requirements.
