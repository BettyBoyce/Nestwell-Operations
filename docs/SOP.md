## 1. Purpose
Nestwell Crew records the work the family crew does for Nestwell Stays LLC:
- **What** was done (jobs, routines, shifts)
- **Proof** that it was done (photos, check-offs, clock times)
- **What each person earned and was paid**

These records support payroll, the bookkeeper and the CPA, and they document real, reasonable work for wages paid to family members. **If it isn't in the app, it didn't happen.** Log the work at the time you do it.

## 4. Crew member procedures

### 4.1 Hourly shifts (Kyle)
**My shifts** shows a calendar of your scheduled days this pay week, with dates (e.g. **Tue 10 · Fri 13**). Today is always included.

**Every scheduled shift (Tuesday & Friday, 10 AM–2 PM):**
1. **When you arrive:** open the app → today's date is already selected → tap **Clock in**. A timer starts and the day shows **On clock**.
2. Do your work.
3. **When you leave:** tap **Clock out**. The day turns green with that day's pay (e.g. **$108**), and the week's total updates.
4. Tap any other day to see it: past days show the hours and pay recorded; upcoming days show your scheduled time. The **Clock in** button only appears on today's date.

**Rules:**
- Clock in **when you start working**, not before, and clock out **before you leave**.
- Only one shift can be open at a time.
- On an unscheduled day, the app shows "Not a scheduled day." Clock in only if a parent asked you to work.
- **Forgot to clock in or out?** Tell a parent right away with your actual times. Only a parent can fix a shift (see 5.5).

**Your dashboard shows:** your schedule and rate, **This week** pay, **Hours this week**, **Owed to you**, and a list of each shift in the pay week (Saturday–Friday).

### 4.2 Daily routines (Landon, Maxine, Renlee)
**My routine** shows a **Monday–Friday calendar with dates** (on weekends it shows the coming week).

**Every routine day (Monday–Friday):**
1. Do your routine task(s).
2. Open the app. **Today's date is already selected.** (To open a different day, tap it on the calendar.)
3. Tap each task **after it's finished**. It turns green with a ✓.
4. When every task for that day is checked, you'll see **"All done for this day. Nice work! ✓"**, the day turns green on the calendar, and a parent is notified.

**Rules:**
- Check a task off **only after it's actually done**.
- **Tapped one by mistake?** Tap it again to uncheck it.
- **Calendar colors:** green ✓ = done, gold = partly done (e.g. 1/2), red = missed, white = upcoming, gray "Off" = no routine that day.
- **Forgot to check off an earlier day?** Tap that day on the calendar and check the tasks. Late check-offs are marked as late on the parent's view.
- You can catch up on any earlier day **this week** (Monday onward). Past weeks need a parent. Future days are view-only until that day.

### 4.3 Jobs and extra jobs (everyone)
Parents post two kinds of jobs:
- **My jobs:** assigned to you
- **Extra jobs — earn more:** open to anyone; the first person to tap **Claim** gets it

**To complete a job:**
1. **Extra job:** tap **Claim**. It moves to **My jobs**.
2. In **My jobs**, follow the steps on the card: **① Clock in ② Do the work ③ Take photos ④ Send for review**
   - **Clock in / Clock out** on a job is optional. Use it for hourly-rate jobs ("$15.00/hr").
3. Tap **Take or choose photo**. Add 1–6 photos of the **finished** work (before and after is great). Tap **×** to remove a photo.
4. Tap **Mark done & send for review**. This button stays gray until a photo is added (unless the job says no photo is needed).
5. The job moves to **Waiting for approval**. You'll get a notification when it's approved, or if it's sent back.

**If a job is sent back:** it returns to **My jobs** with a **Parent note** explaining what to fix. Fix it, take new photos and send it again.

**Good photos:** take them in good light, show the whole area, and take one photo per area when there are several.

## 5. Parent / manager procedures

### 5.1 Daily check (about 2 minutes)
1. Open the app and check 🔔 for new alerts.
2. Look at each crew card on the dashboard:
   - **Kyle:** on the clock? Hours and pay this week.
   - **Landon / Maxine / Renlee:** "Today: all done ✓" or "0 of 1 done," plus the Mon–Fri strip.
3. Clear **Needs your review** (see 5.3).

### 5.2 Posting a job
On the dashboard, under **Post a job**:
1. **Job:** a clear title, ideally with the property (e.g. "Restock coffee @ Salem Unit A")
2. **Details:** what "done" looks like
3. **Category**
4. **Who:** pick a person, or **Anyone — post as extra job**
5. **Pay:** **Flat** (a set amount) or **Hourly** (rate × time clocked on the job), then the **Amount**
6. **Due date** (optional)
7. Photos are **required by default**. Tick **No photo needed** only for jobs that can't be photographed (e.g. "Call the plumber").
8. Tap **Post job & notify**. The assigned person, or everyone for an extra job, is notified.

To cancel a job that isn't approved yet, tap **Remove** under **Open & in progress**.

### 5.3 Reviewing and approving work
Under **Needs your review**:
1. Check the photos. Tap any photo to see it full size.
2. Either:
   - **Approve:** the pay is added to their balance and they're notified. For hourly-rate jobs, pay = hours clocked × rate.
   - **Send back:** type what needs fixing, then tap **Send back**. The job returns to their **My jobs**.

Approve only work you've seen proof of. Approved amounts can't be edited in the app.

### 5.4 Paying people
Pay outside the app (**Venmo, Check, Cash or Payroll**), then **record it in the app** so balances stay correct.
- **Kyle (every Friday):** on his dashboard card, choose the method and tap **Mark $___ paid**. This pays his full balance and notifies him. Do it **after** his Friday shift is clocked out.
- **Anyone else, or a custom amount:** **Team & Pay → Record a payout** → crew member, amount, method, note → **Record payout & notify**.

**Owed** = everything earned (approved jobs + completed shifts) − everything recorded as paid.

> Payroll crew (Landon, Maxine, Renlee) are paid through payroll. The app records their routine check-offs as proof of work, not their payroll amounts. Only record a payout in the app for **extra jobs** they've been paid for.

### 5.5 Fixing a shift (forgot to clock in or out, wrong time)
1. **Team & Pay** → tap **Kyle** → scroll to **This pay week**.
2. Find the shift and correct **Date**, **In** and **Out** (Pacific time). Add a **Note** (e.g. "Forgot to clock out, left at 2:00").
3. Tap **Save**. Pay recalculates automatically.
- **Forgot to clock in entirely:** under **Add a missed shift**, enter the date and the real in/out times, add a note, then tap **Add shift**. Pay is calculated at his hourly rate.
- **Delete shift** removes a shift entered by mistake.
- Shifts from the current and previous pay week can be fixed on this screen.

### 5.6 Managing the crew (Team & Pay)
Tap a person's name to open their settings:
- **Pay setup:**
  - **How they're paid:** Per job / Hourly shifts / Payroll
  - **Hourly rate**, **scheduled days**, **shift start and end** (hourly only)
  - **Position title**
  - Tap **Save pay setup** when done.
- **Daily routine** (payroll): **Add routine task** (choose its days) or **Remove**. Removed tasks keep their history.
- **See [Name]'s week →** opens their weekly activity page (5.7).

**Adding a new crew member:** **Add a crew member** → name, birth year, username, password (6+ characters) → **Add to crew**. Then set up their pay. Usernames must be unique and use only letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores.

### 5.7 Reviewing someone's week
Tap a crew member's **name** (or **See this week →**) on the dashboard. You'll see:
- **Summary:** hours and pay, routine completion, jobs done
- **Routine check-offs:** each day with ✓ and the time checked, ✗ for not done, and late check-offs labeled
- **Shifts:** clock-in and clock-out times, hours, pay and total
- **Jobs:** approved (with photos and pay), waiting for review, in progress
- **Paid this week**

Use **‹ ›** to look back up to 6 weeks. Kyle's week runs **Saturday–Friday** (his pay week); everyone else's runs **Monday–Sunday**.

## 6. Pay rules

| Pay type | Who | How earnings are calculated | When paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hourly** | Kyle | Shift hours × hourly rate, to the minute. The rate is locked at clock-in. | Fridays, for the Saturday–Friday week |
| **Payroll** | Landon, Maxine, Renlee | Through payroll, outside the app. The app records routine completion. | Per payroll schedule |
| **Per job** | Brandon (and extra jobs for anyone) | Flat amount, or hours on the job × job rate, **on approval** | When a payout is recorded |

- All times are **Pacific** (Salem, Oregon).
- Changing someone's hourly rate affects **future** shifts only.
- Extra jobs are paid on top of hourly or payroll pay.

## 7. Records & the CPA export
- **Download:** the **Export** button in the top bar (tablet or computer), or **Team & Pay → Payout history → Download CSV** (appears after the first payout is recorded).
  - For a specific year: `https://nestwell-operations.vercel.app/crew/export?year=2026`
- **Contents** (sorted by date):
  - `JOB`: approved jobs, with hours, rate and amount
  - `SHIFT`: hourly shifts, with in/out times, hours, rate and amount
  - `ROUTINE`: each routine check-off, and whether it was checked the same day
  - `PAYOUT`: each payment, with its method
- Every row includes the person's **position**.
- **Recommended:** download at each month-end and year-end, and send to the bookkeeper or CPA.

## 8. Troubleshooting

### 8.1 Crew member problems
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **"That login or password didn't match"** | Check the username spelling (e.g. `maxineb`, not `maxine`). Passwords are case-sensitive. Still stuck? A parent resets the password (9.4). |
| **Forgot password** | Ask a parent. There's no self-service reset yet; see 9.4. |
| **App looks old or isn't updating** | In a browser, pull down to refresh. For the home screen app, swipe it closed and reopen it. |
| **Kyle: "Your account is not set up for hourly shifts yet"** | Parent: Team & Pay → Kyle → How they're paid = **Hourly shifts**, rate = 27 → **Save**. |
| **"You are already clocked in"** | A shift is still open. Tap **Clock out**, then ask a parent to fix the times if needed. |
| **Forgot to clock out** | Tell a parent the real time you left. The parent fixes the shift (5.5). |
| **No routine shows up** | It may be a non-routine day (e.g. weekend). If it's a weekday, a parent checks Team & Pay → that person → **Payroll** and that the routine has that day selected. |
| **"That day is too far back" / a missed day from last week** | Crew can only catch up within the current week. Tell a parent, who notes it for the records. |
| **Can't tap "Mark done & send for review"** | The job needs at least one photo. Tap **Take or choose photo** first. |
| **Photo won't upload / "Photo upload failed"** | Check the internet connection and try again. Take a new photo instead of using an old one, and try fewer photos at once. |
| **"Job is no longer available"** | Someone else claimed that extra job first. |
| **Claimed job disappeared from Extra jobs** | It's now under **My jobs**. Scroll up. |

### 8.2 Parent problems
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **A crew card shows only Owed/Earned/Hrs (no routine or schedule)** | That person is set to **Per job**. Change their pay type on Team & Pay. |
| **Kyle's week pay looks wrong** | Open his week page (5.7) and check each shift's in/out times. Fix with 5.5. |
| **"Mark paid" button missing** | It only appears when the balance owed is above $0. |
| **Photos missing on a job to review** | The job was marked **No photo needed**, or it was submitted before photos were required. Send it back and ask for photos. |
| **Approved the wrong job / wrong amount** | Approvals can't be undone in the app. Correct the balance by recording an adjusting payout with a note, and tell the administrator. |
| **"That username is taken"** | Choose a different username (e.g. add a number). |
| **Routines, shifts or required photos not working at all** | A database update may not have been run. Check the health page (9.2). |
| **A page shows "Application error" or won't load** | Check the health page (9.2), then Vercel deployments (9.3). |

## 9. System administration
*For the owner or whoever maintains the app.*

### 9.1 Where everything lives
| Piece | Service | Where to manage it |
|---|---|---|
| Code | GitHub | https://github.com/BettyBoyce/Nestwell-Operations |
| Website | Vercel (team **boyce6**) | vercel.com → **nestwell-operations** |
| Database, logins, photos | Supabase (connected through Vercel) | Vercel → project → **Storage** → **Open in Supabase** |

Every change pushed to the `main` branch on GitHub deploys automatically to Vercel within about a minute.

### 9.2 Health check
Open **https://nestwell-operations.vercel.app/api/health**. Every item should be `true`:

| Item | If it's `false` |
|---|---|
| `supabase_url`, `public_key`, `server_key` | Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables: check the Supabase variables exist, then **Redeploy** |
| `database_tables`, `crew_functions`, `photo_bucket` | Run `supabase/migrations/0001_crew.sql` (9.5) |
| `required_photos_update` | Run `0002_required_photos.sql` |
| `shifts_routines_update` | Run `0003_pay_types_shifts_routines.sql` |
| `signup_open: true` | Anyone can create a new family account. Set `ALLOW_SIGNUP` = `false` in Vercel and **Redeploy** (recommended). |

The `notes` list explains anything that's missing. The page never shows passwords or keys.

### 9.3 Vercel: deployments and settings
- **Deployments** tab: each update shows **Ready** (✅) or **Error**. Click a failed one to see its build log.
- **Redeploy:** Deployments → **⋯** on the latest one → **Redeploy**. Needed after changing environment variables.
- **Environment Variables** (Settings): the Supabase keys (added automatically by the Supabase connection) and `ALLOW_SIGNUP`.

### 9.4 Resetting a crew member's password
There's no reset button in the app yet. To reset one:
1. Open Supabase (9.1) → **SQL Editor** → **+ New query**.
2. Paste the following, replacing the **username** and **new password**:
   ```sql
   update auth.users
      set encrypted_password = extensions.crypt('NEWPASSWORD', extensions.gen_salt('bf'))
    where email = 'USERNAME@crew.nestwell.local';
   ```
   For example, `'482915'` and `'maxineb@crew.nestwell.local'`.
3. Click **Run**. It should say "Success. 1 rows affected." If it says **0 rows**, the username is spelled wrong.
4. Give the new password to the crew member in person.

Crew logins are stored as `username@crew.nestwell.local`. Parents' logins are their real email addresses.

**Don't delete a crew member** in Supabase → Authentication to "reset" them. That permanently deletes all of their work and pay history.

### 9.5 Running a database update (migration)
New features sometimes come with a file in `supabase/migrations/`. To run one:
1. Open the file on GitHub → **Raw** → select all → copy.
2. In **Supabase → SQL Editor** (not Vercel's Query box, which can't run these) → **+ New query** → paste → **Run**.
3. Look for **"Success. No rows returned."** If asked about "destructive operations," choose **Run this query**.
4. Check the health page (9.2).

Every migration file is safe to run more than once.

| File | Adds |
|---|---|
| `0001_crew.sql` | Base tables, security rules, photo storage |
| `0002_required_photos.sql` | Required photos, several photos per job |
| `0003_pay_types_shifts_routines.sql` | Pay types, hourly shifts, routines, crew setup |

### 9.6 Security basics
- The Supabase **service role / secret key** gives full access. It lives only in Vercel's settings. Never share it, paste it in chat or commit it to GitHub.
- Crew members can only see their own pay, shifts and routines. They can't approve work, edit amounts or see other families' data. The database enforces these rules, not just the screens.
- Keep `ALLOW_SIGNUP=false` once all accounts exist.
- Consider making the GitHub repository **private**: Settings → General → Change visibility.

### 9.7 Backups
The free Supabase plan has no automatic backups, and it may pause after about a week with no activity (un-pause from the Supabase dashboard). Download the CPA export (Section 7) at least monthly as an offline record. Upgrading Supabase to Pro adds daily backups.
