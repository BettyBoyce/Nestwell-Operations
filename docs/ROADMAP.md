# Roadmap

## Crew
### v0.1 ✅ (this release)
- Parent and kid logins (kids use a username, so they don't need an email)
- Job board: assigned jobs plus claimable extra jobs, flat or hourly pay, categories, due dates
- Time clock with a live timer, proof-photo upload, approve or send back with a note
- In-app notifications (posted, claimed, done, approved, payday)
- Balances, payout log and a yearly CSV export for the CPA

### v0.2: notifications that reach the phone
- [ ] Web push notifications (installed app on iPhone 16.4+ and Android). Needs VAPID keys.
- [ ] Optional text alerts through Twilio for kids without the app installed
- [ ] Weekly summary email to parents (Resend)

### v0.3: motivation
- [ ] Savings goals with a progress bar
- [ ] Badges and streaks ("5 five-star jobs," "Turnover Certified")
- [ ] Skill levels that unlock higher-paying jobs
- [ ] Repeating jobs (every Saturday: check the yard at the Salem unit)

### v0.4: Money Lab
- [ ] Split every paycheck into Spend, Save and Invest
- [ ] Roth IRA growth calculator ("$2,000 a year from age 12 → at 60…")
- [ ] Year-end "W-2 ready" summary for each kid

## Other Nestwell products
- [ ] **Turnover Checklists:** checklist templates for each property, linked to Crew jobs
- [ ] **Inventory & Restock:** per-unit par levels and a shopping list
- [ ] **Guest Welcome Kits:** kit builder and tracking for each stay
- [ ] **Properties:** a shared list of properties that every product uses
