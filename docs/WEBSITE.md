# Website: staynestwell.com

**URL:** https://www.staynestwell.com · **Last reviewed:** November 7, 2026

This is a summary of what the public website says today. It's the reference for how Nestwell describes itself to guests. Update it when the site changes.

## Brand & positioning
- **Tagline:** "Designed for Comfort—Built for Life's Transitions"
- **Nestwell Stays:** furnished month-to-month rentals in Salem, Oregon and the Willamette Valley
- **Identity:** veteran-owned, family-operated, locally managed. "Not a national call center."
- **Based in:** Salem, OR

## Who we serve
| Guest type | Who |
|---|---|
| Traveling medical professionals | Nurses, doctors, therapists and other staff at Salem Health and nearby facilities |
| Families relocating to Oregon | Relocating, buying or building a home, starting a job, or needing flexibility during a move |
| Insurance displacement | Local families displaced by fire, flooding, water damage, mold remediation or other covered claims |
| Corporate & executive housing | Executives, consultants, traveling employees and project teams |

## What every home includes
- Fully furnished and equipped, with a fully stocked kitchen
- High-speed WiFi
- Blackout curtains in every bedroom
- Professionally prepared and cleaned
- Flexible month-to-month stays with no long-term commitment
- "Honest pricing with no surprises" (no rates are published)

## Calls to action
- **Browse Available Spaces:** the only reference to listings. No units are named on the site.
- **Book a Call**
- **Get Your Free Salem Guide:** email signup that delivers an instant PDF covering neighborhoods, hospitals and healthcare, restaurants and coffee, Willamette Valley attractions, pet-friendly options, groceries and gyms, and transition tips

## Text messaging (SMS opt-in)
The guide form has two separate opt-in checkboxes:
1. **Non-marketing:** property details and the tour guide
2. **Marketing:** offers, promotions and discounts

Both support **HELP** and **STOP**. Message and data rates may apply.
